## ZIDAS 2026

🎓 **Institution**: [ZIDAS](https://2026.zidas.org)  
📘 **Course**: Zurich Image and Data Analysis School (ZIDAS) 2026  
👩‍🏫 **Role**: Teaching Assistant, Python day  
🗓️ **Dates**: 5-10 July 2026  

---

I served as a teaching assistant during the **Python day** of **ZIDAS 2026**, a one-week hands-on school in image processing and analysis held at EPFL in Lausanne.

The school is designed for life-science researchers who want practical training in bioimage analysis workflows, with an emphasis on interactive exercises, biologically relevant examples, and reproducible image-analysis practice.

As part of the Python day, I supported participants in learning and applying programming concepts for image analysis in a hands-on setting.

### Highlights
- Teaching assistant for the Python-focused day of ZIDAS 2026.
- Supported hands-on training in image processing and analysis for life-science researchers.
- Contributed to a week-long international school hosted at EPFL in Lausanne, Switzerland.
