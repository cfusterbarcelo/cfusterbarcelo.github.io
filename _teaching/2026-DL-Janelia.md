## Deep Learning for Microscopy Image Analysis

🎓 **Institution**: [Janelia Research Campus (HHMI)](https://www.janelia.org/)  
📘 **Course**: [DL@Janelia 2026](https://dl-janelia.github.io/2026/about/)  
👩‍🏫 **Role**: Teaching Assistant  
🗓️ **Dates**: 4-18 June 2026  
💻 **Exercises repository**: [DL-Janelia-2026](https://github.com/dl-janelia/DL-Janelia-2026)

---

I served as a teaching assistant in the 2026 edition of **Deep Learning for Microscopy Image Analysis**, a two-week hands-on bootcamp designed to help life science researchers learn and apply state-of-the-art deep learning methods for microscopy.

During the **first week**, I supported the practical exercise sessions built around the [DL-Janelia-2026](https://github.com/dl-janelia/DL-Janelia-2026) materials. I taught the first exercise, **Boot**, and helped participants work through several of the later exercises as they developed familiarity with the core tools, workflows, and concepts introduced in the course.

During the **second week**, the course shifted to a project-based format where participants developed analysis pipelines for their own datasets. As a TA, I helped students translate the concepts from the first week into concrete project plans, supporting them with problem formulation, workflow design, and application of the methods learned throughout the bootcamp.

### Highlights
- Taught the first practical exercise, **Boot**, during the hands-on training week.
- Supported participants across multiple deep learning exercises for microscopy image analysis.
- Helped students develop project-specific pipelines for their own biological imaging data during the second week.
