I had the pleasure to attend and give a talk at the Workshop for [Effectively Communicating Bioimage Analysis](https://www.biologists.com/workshops/february-2024/) (12–15 February 2024, Buxted Park, East Sussex, UK), organised by [The Company of Biologists](https://www.biologists.com) and [Focal Plane](https://focalplane.biologists.com). 
In this workshop not only did I have a wonderful time at Buxted Park, but I also had the opportunity to present the [deepImageJ](https://deepimagej.github.io) project and learn from the best in the field.
