In November 2026, I will present the poster **"When open data and code are not enough: a reproducibility case study of a published Drosophila tissue-flow analysis workflow"** at the [Crick BioImage Analysis Symposium (CBIAS) 2026](https://www.crick.ac.uk/whats-on/crick-bioimage-analysis-symposium-2026), held at the Francis Crick Institute in London.

The poster presents a reproducibility study of a published bioimage analysis workflow characterising tissue flow in *Drosophila*. It shows that openly available data and code are not always enough to reproduce published results. The study started at the GloBIAS Bioimage Analysts Workshop and was later published in *Journal of Microscopy*.
