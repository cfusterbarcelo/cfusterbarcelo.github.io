On 22 May 2026, I gave an online talk on **BioImage Model Zoo** as part of the [ZMB-BVC Lunch Seminar Series](https://www.biovisioncenter.uzh.ch/en/news/lunch_seminar_series_hs_2025.html), hosted by the [BioVisionCenter at the University of Zurich](https://www.biovisioncenter.uzh.ch/en.html).

The talk introduced the [BioImage Model Zoo](https://bioimage.io), a community-driven platform for sharing and reusing AI models for bioimage analysis, with a focus on interoperability, accessibility, and practical adoption by imaging researchers.
