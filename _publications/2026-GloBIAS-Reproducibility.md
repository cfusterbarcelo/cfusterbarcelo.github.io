## Assessing the reproducibility of a bioimage analysis workflow characterising tissue flow in Drosophila

**Accepted at**: *Journal of Microscopy* (special issue), 2026  
**Status**: Accepted and currently under formatting; final published version pending  
**Context**: Reproducibility study from the GloBIAS Bioimage Analysts Workshop  
**Authors**: Mélodie Ambroset, Cesar A. Valades Cruz, Libert Brice Tonfack, Rocco D’Antuono, Caterina Fuster-Barceló

This work reports a reproducibility study focused on a bioimage analysis workflow for characterising tissue flow in *Drosophila*. The study was developed in the context of the GloBIAS Bioimage Analysts Workshop and has been accepted for a *Journal of Microscopy* special issue.
