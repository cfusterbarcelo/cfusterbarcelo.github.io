## Crick BioImage Analysis Symposium 2026

On 23–24 November 2026, I will attend the [**Crick BioImage Analysis Symposium (CBIAS) 2026**](https://www.crick.ac.uk/whats-on/crick-bioimage-analysis-symposium-2026) at the **Francis Crick Institute** in **London, UK**, where two of my abstracts have been accepted:

- **Oral presentation (15 min)**: [When do vision foundation models transfer to microscopy and when do they fail?]({{ '/talks/2026-CBIAS-Talk' | relative_url }})
  - Related paper: [Are Vision Foundation Models Foundational for Electron Microscopy Image Segmentation?]({{ '/publication/2026-vfms-electron-microscopy' | relative_url }})
- **Poster**: [When open data and code are not enough: a reproducibility case study of a published Drosophila tissue-flow analysis workflow]({{ '/talks/2026-CBIAS-Poster' | relative_url }})
  - Related paper: [Assessing the reproducibility of a bioimage analysis workflow characterising tissue flow in Drosophila]({{ '/publication/2026-globias-reproducibility' | relative_url }})

### Highlights
- Selected for a 15-minute oral presentation at CBIAS 2026.
- Presenting a poster on reproducibility in bioimage analysis.
- Sharing work on vision foundation models for microscopy and on reproducible bioimage analysis workflows.
