I have been selected to represent the **University of Zurich** at the [**Global Young Scientists Summit (GYSS) 2027**](https://gyss.nrf.gov.sg), which will take place in **Singapore** from 4 to 8 January 2027.

The GYSS is an annual summit organised by the [National Research Foundation (NRF) Singapore](https://www.nrf.gov.sg). It brings together young researchers from around the world with Nobel laureates and other eminent scientists to discuss how research and innovation can address major global challenges.

### Highlights
- Selected as a University of Zurich representative.
- Five days of panel huddles, quickfire pitches, fireside chats, and site visits, alongside Nobel laureates and eminent scientists.
